📋 What is ApexFlow?

ApexFlow Business Manager provides a central hub for small business owners. You can handle daily tasks, change system settings, manage software updates, and view helpful guides from one single interface. The application runs through your web browser to provide a familiar experience while you organize your accounts, inventory, and staff schedules.

💻 System Requirements

To run this software, your computer must meet these basic standards:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • Memory: At least 4 gigabytes of RAM.
  • Storage: 200 megabytes of free space.
  • Browser: Any recent version of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.

⚙️ Installation Steps

Follow these steps to set up the software on your Windows computer:

  1. Visit the official download page to see all available versions.
  2. Select the file ending in .exe for the latest release.
  3. Wait for the download to finish.
  4. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the file to start the installation.
  5. Follow the instructions on your screen.
  6. Once the installation completes, the ApexFlow icon will appear on your desktop.

🚀 Getting Started

After you open the program, the main dashboard appears. This dashboard lists your primary business tasks. You can click on any icon to open a specific tool. If you need help, look for the Guidance tab located at the top of the menu bar. This section contains articles and video tutorials to help you understand how each feature works.

🔄 Managing Updates

Your business needs tools that stay current. ApexFlow checks for updates every time you open the app. If a new version exists, the app notifies you. Choose to install the update immediately to ensure you have the latest improvements and security patches. Keeping your software current prevents errors and keeps your data safe.

🛠 Changing Settings

You can adjust the application to fit your workflow. Open the Settings menu to change your notification preferences, update your business name, or adjust your data backup path. Every setting change saves automatically when you click the back button.

💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection? Yes, the platform syncs with your online business account to keep data consistent across all your devices.

Can I use this on a Mac? Currently, this installer is for Windows only. Check the updates section for future support.

Where does the software store my data? It stores your primary data securely in your local business folder, with automatic encrypted backups to our cloud server.
